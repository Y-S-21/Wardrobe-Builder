# Wardrobe Builder — Vision Document

## 1. Introduction
Wardrobe Builder is envisioned as a multi-platform fashion ecosystem that integrates wardrobe management, AI-driven styling, social inspiration, and business integrations. The long-term goal is to become a **Personal Style OS** for individuals, a **Professional Styling Platform** for stylists, and a **Digital Fashion Hub** for brands.
